Scientist, engineer, and investor working at the edges of biology and computation.

I’m a Biophysics PhD student at UC Berkeley in Jennifer Doudna’s lab, and a Venture Partner at Compound. Previously, I worked on machine learning and biology at Google X and Herophilus.

Now

I’m interested in computational biology, protein evolution, and the interfaces between models and molecules. I care about building methods that are statistically sound, useful to experimentalists, and capable of helping us understand biology at scale.

Beyond the lab, I spend time with people building ambitious companies in biotechnology and other areas of frontier technology.
