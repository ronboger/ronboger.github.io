Hi, I’m Ron.

I spend most of my time trying to understand biology with computers, statistics, and whatever else seems useful. Right now I’m doing a Biophysics PhD at UC Berkeley in Jennifer Doudna’s lab. I also spend time with early-stage companies as a Venture Partner at Compound.

Before Berkeley, I worked on machine learning and biology at Google X and Herophilus.

what I’m doing now

In the lab, I’m broadly interested in things at the intersection of computation, physics, and biotechnology. Lately that means protein evolution, sequence and structure, and building tools that are actually useful to experimentalists.

More generally, Berkeley feels like home after a few years of bouncing around. I’m always looking to meet introspective, curious, and kind people on the journey.
