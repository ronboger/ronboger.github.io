Hey there! My name is Ron.

I spend most of my time between science, startups, and art. I'm doing a Biophysics PhD at UC Berkeley in [Jennifer Doudna's lab](https://doudnalab.org/), where I work on problems at the intersection of computation, physics, and biotechnology. I also work with early-stage companies as a Venture Partner at [Compound](https://www.compound.vc/).

Previously, I worked on machine learning and biology at [Google X](https://x.company/) and [Herophilus](https://www.herophilus.com/), and did research in the [VisionLab](http://vision.jhu.edu/) at Johns Hopkins.

I studied Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, and Mathematics at [Johns Hopkins](https://www.jhu.edu/). While I was there, I founded and directed [MedHacks](https://medhacks.org/), was a KPCB Fellow, and studied piano performance at the Peabody Conservatory.

I was born in Tel Aviv and grew up in Baltimore. I've also spent time living in San Francisco, Kauai, Lisbon, Buenos Aires, and Neot Semedar. I love languages—at one point I spoke English, Hebrew, Spanish, Portuguese, and French, although the last two are probably rusty.

Feel free to reach out.
